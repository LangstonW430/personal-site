Maria's Hair Emporium needed a site that read as considered rather than templated — the salon's whole pitch is craft and attention, and a generic small-business theme would have undercut that on arrival. The type system leans on four serif and display faces picked for warmth over neutrality, a deliberate departure from the mono-and-one-serif system this register itself uses; a client site earns its own voice.

Structure is deliberately simple: services, a gallery, staff, and a contact page that routes straight to a phone call, because that's how this business actually books appointments. Nothing here needed a CMS or a database — the whole site is static, and updates happen by editing content and redeploying.

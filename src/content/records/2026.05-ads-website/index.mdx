Nine pages for a commercial HVAC air-distribution parts distributor, built around one goal: make it easy and credible to invite the company to bid on a job. Capability statement, product lines, a twenty-three-entry brand roster — Modine, Mestek, Hart & Cooley, Krueger, DuraVent, Senneca — and a request-a-bid form posting to Netlify behind a honeypot field. Every page ladders to the bid rather than trying to be a general-purpose company brochure.

Brand color, type and spacing live in `src/styles/tokens.css`, read by the Tailwind config, so re-skinning the site is a one-file edit rather than a find-and-replace across templates. The file is labelled a placeholder in its own header: the values were sampled off the client's logo because no brand guide exists yet, and the comment says so rather than letting a guess quietly harden into a decision. Header and hero heights both derive from one `--logo-height` custom property with height-based media queries, so the hero survives a short window instead of being eaten by the bar above it.

Content runs through Sveltia CMS on a GitHub backend — the client edits copy and swaps photography without a database or a hosting bill for a CMS backend. Real assets are landing incrementally as they arrive.

Astro 5 with no CSS framework — the stylesheet is hand-written, which for a designer's own site was the point. Nothing in the layout is a default anyone would recognize on sight.

Thirteen projects across design, art and photography, typed through Astro content collections and opening from the work grid into their own pages behind a lightbox. The work is real client work — the Eastman Museum, Brockport, ColorStack's Black Enterprise edition, a St. Anne's Family Services holiday card — so the grid had to hold a print piece and a photo series side by side without either reading as a mistake.

Content runs through Sveltia CMS at `/admin`, committing to `main` on GitHub with Netlify Identity handling auth. Every page is editable, not just the project list: hero copy, both about sections, the contact heading, every image. That was as much the deliverable as the site — KJ files new work without touching code and without paying for a CMS backend.

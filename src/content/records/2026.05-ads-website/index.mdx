This is a nine-page bid-focused site for a commercial HVAC parts distributor, built around one goal: make it easy and credible to invite the company to bid on a job. Every page — capability statement, brand roster, product lines, contact — ladders up to that, rather than trying to be a general-purpose company brochure.

Brand colors live as CSS custom properties that the Tailwind config reads, so re-skinning the whole site is a one-file edit rather than a find-and-replace across templates. Content runs through a Git-backed CMS admin — the client edits copy and swaps photos without a database or a hosting bill for a CMS backend.

Real content — logo, hero photography, product photos, brand roster — is landing incrementally as the client provides it; the team directory and testimonials are still placeholders on their end, tracked in the repo's own checklist rather than guessed at here.

The salon's whole pitch is craft and attention, and a generic small-business theme would have undercut that on arrival. The type system runs four self-hosted faces — Italiana, Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond and Lora — picked for warmth over neutrality, a deliberate departure from the mono-and-one-serif system this register itself uses. A client site earns its own voice.

Seven pages: home, services, gallery, about, contact, a workshop page, and a styleguide kept in the build so the type and color decisions stay checkable against the thing they were decided for. The gallery is twenty-one entries in an Astro content collection across cuts, color and extensions, each its own markdown file, opened through a lightbox. Services are eight categories in one JSON file. Nothing needed a CMS or a database; updates are a content edit and a redeploy.

The contact page took its structure from how the business actually books. Appointments go through Square, so that link and the phone number lead, rather than sitting under a general-purpose form. The forms that exist are split by service, and the extension inquiry accepts a photo upload, because that consultation cannot start without one.
